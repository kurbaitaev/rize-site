Privacy Policy for Rize

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

Rize does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no servers. Everything the app knows about you stays on your iPhone.

What Rize accesses, and why

Location. Rize uses your device's location to calculate the correct Fajr prayer time for where you are. Prayer times are computed entirely on your device using the standard adhan calculation library. To show your city's name, your coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service in a request handled by iOS — the same lookup Maps and Weather use. They are never sent to us, because there is no "us" to send them to: Rize has no server. Your location is stored only on your device so the app can work without asking again.

Camera. During the exercise challenge that dismisses the alarm, Rize uses the front camera to count your repetitions through on-device pose detection. The video never leaves your phone. No images or video are recorded, saved, or transmitted. The camera is active only while you are on the exercise screen.

Alarms. Rize schedules alarms through Apple's AlarmKit, so iOS itself rings the alarm — even when the app is closed or your phone is silenced. Alarms are created and delivered entirely by iOS on your phone. There is no push server and nothing is sent over the internet.

Motion. If you choose the motion-sensor mode for dismissing the alarm, the accelerometer is read on-device to count your movements. Motion data is never stored or transmitted.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (calculation method, madhab, alarm and exercise preferences), your exercise calibration, and your most recent location. All of it lives in your app's private storage and is removed when you delete the app.

What is not collected

No personal information. No usage analytics. No advertising identifiers. No crash reporting to third parties. No data sold or shared with anyone, because none is gathered in the first place.

Children

Rize is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Retention and deletion

Rize retains nothing about you beyond the on-device settings above. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server-side data to request or erase, and no consent to revoke beyond the iOS permissions, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date above revised.
