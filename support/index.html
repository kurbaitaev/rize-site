What Rize does

Rize is a Fajr alarm that keeps coming back until you have completed an exercise challenge. It calculates the Fajr time for your location on your device and wakes you with real system alarms (Apple's AlarmKit) that ring even in Silent mode - at Fajr, then again at 3, 6, 10, 15, 20 and 30 minutes. To dismiss the morning you complete squats, counted by the camera, use the motion sensor, or solve math or geography questions. Holding "Hold for 5 quiet minutes" on the challenge screen pauses the alarm for five minutes; it then rings again, and keeps ringing until the challenge is done or the alarm is switched off on the home screen.

Rize requires iOS 26.

The alarm did not go off

Check these in order:

  1. Alarms must be allowed. Rize asks for alarm permission during setup; if it was denied, the home screen shows a warning - tap it, or allow alarms for Rize in iOS Settings. Without this permission no alarm can ring.
  2. Alarm Enabled must be on. Settings inside Rize → Alarm → Alarm Enabled.
  3. Location must be allowed, otherwise the Fajr time cannot be calculated and the countdown will show --:--. iOS Settings → Rize → Location.
  4. Check diagnostics. Settings → Alarm diagnostics shows, in plain language, whether iOS is holding your alarms and when they will ring.
  5. Test it. Settings → Alarm → Test Alarm fires real alarms - in 15 seconds, then again after 1 and 2 minutes - so you do not have to wait until dawn.
  6. Don't hide Rize. System alarms fail silently for apps that are hidden or set to Require Face ID in iOS Settings. Keep Rize visible.

The alarm rings quietly

Rize's alarms ring through the silent switch (that is the point of them), but they play at your Ringtone and Alerts volume. Check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone and Alerts. Each ring lasts about half a minute; the alarm then returns a few minutes later rather than ringing continuously.

The prayer time looks wrong

Settings → Prayer Calculation lets you pick from twelve calculation methods and choose your madhab (Shafi'i or Hanafi). Different local authorities use different conventions; pick the one your community follows.

The camera does not count my reps

Prop the phone so your whole body is in frame and there is reasonable light. Recalibrating from the home screen (Calibrate) also helps. If you use the motion sensor instead, Settings → Exercise Detection → Motion sensor sensitivity adjusts how readily a rep is counted.
